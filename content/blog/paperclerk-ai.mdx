Wednesday: "Just in case..." *download*

Thursday: "Wait, didn't I already...?"

Friday: 500 PDFs stare back, unread.

If this feels familiar, same. That's why I built PaperClerk AI, a lightweight, local, AI-powered assistant that keeps a research library under control.

## What it does

It finds the PDFs scattered across your machine and summarizes them with a local LLaMA model (via [Ollama](https://ollama.com/)), so you can see what each paper is about without guessing from the filename. You can set it to auto-keep, auto-skip, or auto-archive papers based on your interests, and it emails you a digest every day or week. Everything runs locally, so your library stays private.

If your "Read Later" folder has become a PDF graveyard, this might help.

Check it out on [GitHub](https://github.com/MoMoDawoud/PaperClerk).
