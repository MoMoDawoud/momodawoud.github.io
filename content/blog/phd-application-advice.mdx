When I was preparing my own PhD applications, I was fortunate to receive guidance from people who generously shared their time and experience during that stressful period. I've tried to keep up that habit of giving back, and this year I'd like to continue.

I'll be offering personalized feedback and advice to a group of applicants applying mainly to PhD programs in Computer Science or related fields.

I can help with application materials (SoP, research statement, CV), defining your research focus and choosing programs, and overall application strategy.

Since my time is limited, I won't be able to respond to everyone individually. I'll select a group of applicants based on their level of preparation and alignment with my expertise, so I can give useful guidance.

If you're applying this year and would like to be considered, please fill out [this short form](https://forms.gle/vv5HXf15Fu3ZthQr5). You can also read [how I approach mentoring](/service#mentorship).
